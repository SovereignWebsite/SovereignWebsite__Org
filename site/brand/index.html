Logo and mark

The Sovereign Website logo is a website window whose top edge rises into a crown: a site that belongs to its owner. It comes as a square mark and as a wordmark. The letters are drawn as shapes, so the files need no font and load nothing from anywhere else.

Wordmark, for light backgrounds. Download the SVG sovereign-website-wordmark.svg

Wordmark, for dark navy, including the top of a vertical 720 by 1280 video frame. Download the SVG sovereign-website-wordmark-navy.svg

Square mark, for icons, avatars and small spaces. Download the SVG sovereign-website-mark.svg

See three more variations: SW crowned, SWS, and the sovereign shield.

Using it

  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the crown clear on every side.
  • Smallest size: the mark at 16 pixels, the wordmark at 20 pixels tall.
  • In a 720 by 1280 frame: the navy wordmark about 360 pixels wide (half the frame), centred, with 56 pixels above it.
  • Do not stretch it, recolour it, add effects to it, or set the name in another typeface beside the mark.

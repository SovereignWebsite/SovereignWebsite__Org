Logo and mark

Logo variations

Three more directions for the Sovereign Website logo, beside the first. The name stays Sovereign Website in every one; the marks play with the letters SW and SWS and with what "sovereign" means. Each comes as a square mark and as a wordmark for light and for navy backgrounds, as SVG with the letters drawn as shapes.

First design: the crowned window

A. SW, crowned

A bold S and a green W under one small crown: the initials of Sovereign Website, and of the sovereign web.

Navy wordmark (SVG)
Square mark (SVG)

B. SWS

Sovereign Website, shortened to SWS. The green W stands between the two S like a crown, over a single green line.

Navy wordmark (SVG)
Square mark (SVG)

C. The sovereign shield

A crowned shield with a bold S: a site that is yours to defend, as well as to own.
